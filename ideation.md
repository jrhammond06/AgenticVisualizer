# Things to think about 
## What does the referee DO?
Right now referee is purely assessing and concluding - decides if "consensus is reached" based on the conversation so far. Does it ever enforce rules? Interact directly with agents? Course-correct the topic of conversation? 

Another way to put it: is the referee related to how the system *communicates and/or enforces* its rules, or is the referee a passive monitor that evaluates conclusions?

And a possible middle ground(?): does the referee *act* in any way? For example, if we have a "majority vote" system that is code-implemented (meaning it's not just "ask the agents to vote"), is the referee in charge of observing and tallying the votes and determining the winner? What if an agent cheats, or if something goes wrong? Whose job is it to identify the issue and/or step in?
